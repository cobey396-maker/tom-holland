# Safety

- **Never aim at people, animals, or faces.** Eye protection on during every test.
- **Low pressure only.** No CO2 cartridges or high-pressure gas.
- **Phases 1–3 chemistry** (alginate, calcium chloride) is food-grade, but still: no ingesting, gloves recommended, calcium chloride irritates eyes.
- **Acetone (Phase 4) is highly flammable.** Only in a lab with a fume hood and supervision, never near motors, sparks, or batteries outside that setup.
- **Dopamine, silk processing reagents (e.g. lithium bromide):** lab only, follow the lab's safety training and SDS sheets.
- Keep electronics and fluids separated; liquids near a battery are a short-circuit risk.
