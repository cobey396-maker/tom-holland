# Brainstorm & open questions

Decisions we still need to make. Write answers inline as we go.

## 1. How is the fluid pushed?
- **Stepper syringe pump** — precise, easy to control and measure; slow-ish, bulky
- **Pressurized reservoir** (hand pump / small air tank + valve) — fast "shot", harder to meter
- **Spring-loaded syringe** — simple, one shot per cock, no electronics

## 2. One shot or continuous stream?
- Single "thwip" burst vs. holding the trigger for a continuous line

## 3. Where do the fluids live?
- Cartridges on the wrist (swappable, like the comics) vs. backpack/belt with tubing

## 4. Trigger
- Classic two-finger palm tap (pressure sensor or button)
- Arm/safe switch required before firing

## 5. Nozzle
- DIY coaxial from two blunt needles + a T/Y luer fitting vs. a 3D-printed nozzle body
- Clogging: alginate gels the moment it touches calcium — how do we keep the tip clean between shots?

## 6. What makes our version stand out?
- Measured performance data, not just a video
- Open build guide anyone can replicate cheaply
- Ideas: retractable/cut-off mechanism, multiple "web modes" (thin line vs. wide splat), app that logs each shot

## 7. Success criteria for mid-November
- e.g. "a continuous fiber ≥ X cm forms in mid-air and sticks to a target Y cm away, ≥ Z% of shots"
